When Was That?

Show a screenshot from your chats. Guess the day it happened somewhere between the first message and today.

1. The timeline

2. The screenshots

Add screenshots, then tap each one to set the day it happened. You can also draw black boxes over anything that gives the answer away, like WhatsApp's date chips ("Today", "12 March") or the clock in the status bar.

3. The board

Like Jeopardy: a column per category and a row per point value. Put easier screenshots on the low values and harder ones on the high values. Tap a square to put a screenshot in it.

4. Play

Scoring: the closer the guess, the more of the question's points she gets. Exact day = all points. Half points when off by days.

Backup & move devices

Everything is saved in this browser only. To prepare the quiz on one phone and play it on another, export it to a file and import it on the other device.
